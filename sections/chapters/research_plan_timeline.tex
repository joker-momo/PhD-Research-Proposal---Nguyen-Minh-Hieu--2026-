\section*{Research Plan and Timeline}
This research is organized into three phases over 36 months. The timeline is designed to support the submission of three research papers and the completion of the PhD thesis. Paper milestones denote \emph{submission} dates rather than acceptance: Papers~1--2 target ranked conferences or magazine venues with faster review turnaround, Paper~3 targets a journal, and each subsequent research phase runs in parallel with the preceding paper's review--revision cycle so that reviewer feedback is absorbed without blocking progress.

\textbf{Year 1: Foundational Work and Core Partitioning Architecture (Months 1-12)} \\
The primary objective of the first year is to establish the theoretical and methodological foundation for RQ1.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Phase 1: Foundational Research and Environment Setup (Months 1-8)}
    \begin{itemize}
      \item \textit{Tasks:} Conduct a comprehensive literature review on Split Learning, FANETs, and MAS. Establish the experimental and simulation environment using Python with PyTorch and TensorFlow for AI models, plus NS-3 and Gazebo for UAV networking and physics.
      \item \textit{Deliverables:} A detailed literature review chapter; A functional co-simulation environment.
    \end{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Phase 2: Design and Implementation of the Computational Layer \& First Paper (Months 9-12)}
    \begin{itemize}
      \item \textit{Tasks:} Formulate and analyze the dynamic model partitioning problem under bounded-$K$ constraints. Perform controlled evaluation to assess latency and memory implications of candidate formulations. Begin drafting the first research paper.
      \item \textit{Deliverables:} A formal problem formulation and analysis report; A technical report on partition benchmarks.
      \item \textit{Milestone:} Submission of the first research paper on joint partitioning--assignment optimization (Target: Month 12).
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Year 2: Network Layer, System Integration, and Publications (Months 13-24)} \\
The second year focuses on formulating and evaluating communication models and validating coupled optimization behavior.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Phase 3: Development of the Tensor Communication Protocol (Months 13-18)}
    \begin{itemize}
      \item \textit{Tasks:} Formulate and evaluate FANET communication models tailored for tensor exchange. Analyze intermediate feature compression effects on bandwidth, delay, and RF energy.
      \item \textit{Deliverables:} Communication-cost models and analysis results; Empirical data on RF energy consumption per inference cycle.
    \end{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Phase 4: Integrated Evaluation and Second Paper Preparation (Months 19-24)}
    \begin{itemize}
      \item \textit{Tasks:} Validate the coupled partitioning--communication formulations through integrated co-simulation. Measure end-to-end inference latency and total energy cost under representative mobility conditions.
      \item \textit{Deliverables:} A comprehensive validation report on coupled optimization outcomes.
      \item \textit{Milestone:} Submission of the second research paper on communication-aware optimization over FANETs (Target: Month 24).
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Year 3: Adaptive Orchestration, Holistic Evaluation, and Thesis (Months 25-36)} \\
The final year is dedicated to online adaptation analysis, holistic validation, and thesis completion.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Phase 5: Development of the Control Plane and Full System Evaluation (Months 25-30)}
    \begin{itemize}
      \item \textit{Tasks:} Formulate and evaluate online re-optimization policies using real-time UAV telemetry. Analyze fault-tolerance behavior through dynamic task reallocation under simulated node dropouts and battery failures.
      \item \textit{Deliverables:} Validated online adaptation algorithms; Analysis report on resilience and continuity.
      \item \textit{Milestone:} Submission of the third research paper detailing adaptive online re-optimization under failures (Target: Month 30).
    \end{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Phase 6: Thesis Writing and Defense (Months 31-36)}
    \begin{itemize}
      \item \textit{Tasks:} Consolidate all research findings and published papers into the final PhD thesis document. Prepare for the final thesis defense.
      \item \textit{Deliverables:} Submitted PhD thesis.
      \item \textit{Final Milestone:} Successful PhD thesis defense.
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}
