# PRISMA 2020 checklist for the HK261 comparison manuscript

Page references are one-based physical pages of the [current 30-page comparison PDF](../../manuscript/sn_computer_science/main.pdf). Status describes what the review reports and identifies where methods or evidence are partial, unperformed or awaiting confirmation.
