# Project Charter

## Purpose

Complete the HK261 scientific-methods assignment through a truthful,
reproducible PRISMA 2020 systematic review and an actual journal-submission
workflow. Journal acceptance is not required. This is coursework and is separate
from the PhD dissertation roadmap.

## Central review idea

Systematically review how collaborative DNN inference is partitioned,
orchestrated, and made resilient in UAV swarms, and derive evidence-bounded
requirements for reproducible failure-aware UAV-edge systems.

Working title:

> Collaborative Neural Network Inference in Unmanned Aerial Vehicle Swarms: A
> Systematic Review of Partitioning, Orchestration, and Resilience

The review questions and eligibility criteria are canonical in `../protocol.md`
and its dated amendments. Do not silently change them. Any change requires a dated,
justified protocol amendment with its effect on completed work.

## Official coursework obligations

- Use approximately 20 or more relevant journal/conference papers to develop one
  central scientific-paper idea.
- Produce a scientific manuscript containing at least Title, Abstract,
  Introduction, Results, Conclusions, and References, plus all sections required
  by the selected template.
- Select a specialist journal, use its official template, and actually submit
  through its online system.
- Do not fabricate data or represent unperformed work as observed results.
- Use the verified HCMUT email. Nguyen Minh Hieu must be first author or
  corresponding author, and the supervisor must be in the author list.
- Archive the manuscript ID and receipt, then forward evidence to
  `duyanhnguyen@hcmut.edu.vn` with subject
  `Bài tập lớn – Tiểu luận lớp PPNCKH HK261`, name, verified doctoral student ID,
  manuscript ID, and proof of receipt.
- The deadline is three days before the examination. The examination date is
  unknown and must never be guessed.

## Direct user decisions

- Review type: systematic review reported with PRISMA 2020.
- Publication window: 1 January 2021 through the final search date.
- Corpus-expansion target: 30-35 eligible primary studies only if supported by
  evidence; never pad the corpus to reach a number.
- Active venue: SN Computer Science.
- Canonical manuscript format: LaTeX compiled to PDF. Word files are historical
  unless the user explicitly requests Word.
- Codex owns writing, editing, acceptance, submission preparation, and Git.
  Research agents return structured evidence only.

## Current completion boundary

The project is not submission-ready merely because an interim PDF builds. Formal
database exports and final PRISMA reconciliation, additional eligible studies,
human adjudication, author metadata/approval, declarations, and actual submission
evidence remain separate gates.
