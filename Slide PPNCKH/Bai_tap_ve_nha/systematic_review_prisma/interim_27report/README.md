The canonical working corpus is 27 reports = 24 included + 1 excluded demo (S03) + 2 uncertain reports (S19 publication type; C06 inference-time orchestration). Full-text formats are 25 PDFs and two HTML reports. This is a managed report corpus, not a final database-level PRISMA flow.

It studies simulated collaborative Llama-2 inference, with contiguous layer/device mapping, sequence partitioning and token speculation. Token rollback is not failed-UAV recovery. Comparative numerical effects from the seven expansion reports are excluded from synthesis.

## Appraisal boundary
