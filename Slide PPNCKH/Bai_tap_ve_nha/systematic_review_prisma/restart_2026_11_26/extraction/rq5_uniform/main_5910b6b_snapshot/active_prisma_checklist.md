# PRISMA 2020 checklist for the 46-study HK261 review

The locators refer to physical pages of the [verified 16-page manuscript](../../manuscript/sn_computer_science/main.pdf) and the associated supplements. Status and coverage record where reporting is complete, partial, or pending author confirmation.

The [active reconciliation](active_reconciliation_review.md) fixes the included report and study counts. The [Desk Reject revision evidence](../synthesis/desk_reject_revision_review.md) traces the new synthesis and source checks.
