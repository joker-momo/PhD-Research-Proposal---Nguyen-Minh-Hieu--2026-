# Coursework review limitations

- **Coverage.** IEEE Xplore, Scopus and ACM Digital Library were core sources; OpenAlex was supplementary. Web of Science remained outside the search because access was unavailable. The ACM collection used manual result capture and a month-level end filter, followed by downstream eligibility checks.
- **Unavailable and pending evidence.** Of 137 reports sought, 93 were not retrieved through the logged routes. Of 44 assessed full texts, 13 were excluded, six await classification, and 25 were included. Pending cases comprise five publication-date decisions and one publication-type decision. No pending report contributes to the synthesis.
- **Review roles.** Gemini.app provided structured assistance and Codex checked accepted work against the sources. There was no documented independent human duplicate screening or adjudication.
- **Quantitative interpretation.** The paper describes study-specific outcomes and comparison settings. P08 preserves 58 traceable quantitative rows and withholds 11. Selection of two illustrative outcomes is not an aggregate comparison. The engineering appraisal covers ten domains; no formal certainty grade, reporting-bias assessment, meta-analysis or sensitivity analysis was conducted.
- **Publication status.** Post-publication checks are time- and source-bounded. A missing notice from a checked source cannot establish that one does not exist. See the integrity audit for actual results and coverage.
- **Author and dissemination status.** Author order and consent, affiliation, correspondence, contributions, funding and conflicts still need author confirmation. The package is a local coursework handoff. It contains no raw database dumps or article PDFs. No journal submission or forwarding email was performed.
