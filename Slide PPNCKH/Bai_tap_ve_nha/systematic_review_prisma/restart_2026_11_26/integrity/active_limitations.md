# HK261 46-study coursework handoff limitations

- R0081 and R0082 await confirmation of eligible publication status (E7). R0132 awaits evidence on the evaluated system and collaborative inference (E2/E3). These three reports were assessed and await classification.
- R0116 is the sole unretrieved report. Zenodo record 20404256 supplied a source-code ZIP. The archive inspection found no article PDF. The supplemental retrieval recovered 92 of the 93 originally missing reports, giving 136 assessed reports overall.
- The four-source search includes the recorded ACM month-level filter and a downstream exact cutoff. Web of Science was inaccessible. No citation chaining contributed records. No public protocol registration is documented.
- AI-assisted selection, extraction and appraisal were checked against retained evidence. Independent duplicate human screening or extraction and author adjudication remain undocumented.
- The ten-domain engineering appraisal describes quality and completeness of evaluation and reporting. Formal risk of bias due to missing results, a separate study-level risk-of-bias assessment, sensitivity analysis and outcome-level certainty grading were not performed.
- The 67 retained outcome entries come from 25 of the 46 included studies and preserve source conditions. Three controlled within-study contrasts illustrate specific decisions. Heterogeneous networks, hardware, tasks and comparators limit inference across studies. Test conditions for simulated failure, bench disconnection and planned handoff support different conclusions. No pooled effect or ranking across studies was computed.
- R0113 is identifiable by publisher page and original PDF. The publisher's DOI string is incomplete and the bibliography leaves the DOI field empty. Notice checks cover dated Crossref and Retraction Watch evidence, plus the R0113 publisher record. Publisher notice coverage is not exhaustive.
- Author order, names, affiliations, contacts, ORCIDs, contributions, funding and funder role, conflicts, permissions, data access and final AI disclosure await author confirmation. The package contains derived coursework materials. Redistribution of source PDFs or database exports requires a separate rights determination.
- The Springer Nature LaTeX and PDF package is for the HK261 coursework handoff. Journal submission and external email are outside this phase.
