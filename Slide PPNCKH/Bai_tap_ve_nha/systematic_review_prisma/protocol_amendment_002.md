# Protocol Amendment 002

**Date:** 2026-11-24  
**Applies to:** Protocol version 0.1 after Amendment 001  
**Timing:** After pilot calibration and before formal database searches, final screening, extraction of the complete corpus, or evidence synthesis.

## Change

The lower publication-date boundary is changed from 1 January 2015 to **1 January 2021**. Eligible reports must therefore be published from 2021 through the final search date.

This restriction applies to:

1. database interface filters and translated queries;
2. title/abstract and full-text eligibility decisions;
3. the Methods and Abstract of the manuscript;
4. the evidence tables and narrative synthesis.

Records published before 2021 may remain in raw retrieval and pilot-screening files for auditability, but they are ineligible and must not contribute evidence to the systematic review.

## Reason

The user specified that the review must cover research published from 2021 onward. The boundary also focuses the review on the recent development of collaborative and distributed DNN inference in UAV swarms.

## Effect on completed work

No formal core-database search or final PRISMA screening had been completed when the boundary was changed. The 20 priority reports already selected all fall within 2021–2026, so no priority report is removed. Earlier pilot records are retained but will be excluded by publication year during final screening.
