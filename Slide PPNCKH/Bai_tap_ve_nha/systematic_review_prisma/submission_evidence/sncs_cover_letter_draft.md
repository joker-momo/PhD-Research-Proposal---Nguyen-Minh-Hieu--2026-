Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript,“Collaborative Neural Network Inference in Unmanned Aerial Vehicle Swarms:A Systematic Review of Partitioning,Orchestration,and Resilience,”for publication in SN Computer Science.

The manuscript examines how inference workloads are divided and scheduled across systems containing multiple unmanned aerial vehicles. Its evidence map separates model-cut selection,layer-to-host mapping,migration of fixed modules and whole-model request scheduling. It also distinguishes analytical reliability constraints and proactive migration from recovery demonstrated under node unavailability.

The current working synthesis contains23 studies from26 assessed full-text reports. It compares evaluation methods,outcome definitions,network assumptions,uncertainty reporting and reproducibility evidence. Its contribution is an explicit account of what the inspected evidence supports and which comparisons remain necessary;it does not present a new validated inference algorithm or a pooled performance estimate.

The manuscript connects distributed computing,edge intelligence,wireless communication and aerial systems. It derives requirements for controlled comparisons of communication cost,model placement and inference continuity. Proposed benchmark requirements are identified as future work.
